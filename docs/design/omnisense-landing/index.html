A CLEAR VIEW OF THE DEMONSTRATION

One zone. Two sensors.
The whole decision trail.

What can I explore?+

Current temperature, humidity and occupancy; individual sensor evidence; forecast windows and planned restarts; historical cooling relationships; and searchable, exportable operating records.

Is this connected to real cooling equipment?+

No. This demonstration uses synthetic operating records and simulated reported cooling outputs. It does not actuate physical HVAC equipment.

Does it measure energy savings?+

No. The analysis shows requested operating durations and their relationship to occupancy. It does not measure electricity consumption, and energy savings have not been experimentally verified.
